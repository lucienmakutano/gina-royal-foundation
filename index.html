Welcome to GINA Royal Foundation

We serve widows in Machakos County, Kenya, specifically working with 20 women in the small village of Malindi.

Imperfect vessels do great stuff.

Inspired by the art of Kintsugi—repairing broken pottery with golden lacquer to create unique beauty—we help women find their strength and beauty from their brokenness. Just as Kintsugi celebrates the golden journey of repair, we celebrate the golden journey of life.

Our Core Values

  • Hope - An anchor and motivator to move forward
  • Resilience - Inner strength to rise from the ashes
  • Courage - To accept, heal, and defy the odds
  • Empowerment - Building sustainable futures

What We Do

We create sustainable change through:

  • Sustainable farming and kitchen gardens
  • Educational support for children
  • Water access infrastructure
  • Traditional basket weaving training
  • Healthcare and counseling services

A Multigenerational Approach

GRF serves women from age 30 to 90+ years old, spanning 4 generations along with their children and grandchildren. This transgenerational approach ensures lasting change that extends through families and communities for years to come.
