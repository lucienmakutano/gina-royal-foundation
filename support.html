Support Our Mission

Your support creates lasting change for widows and their families. Partner with us to build sustainable futures and transform entire communities.

Your support can make a real difference in the lives of widows and their families in Machakos County, Kenya.

Why Your Support Matters

Every contribution to GINA Royal Foundation creates ripples of change that extend far beyond individual widows. When you support our work, you're not just helping one woman—you're uplifting an entire family and contributing to the transformation of a whole community.

By uplifting a woman, we uplift an entire community.

Partnership Opportunities

Sustainable Farming

Opportunity: Partner with us on sunflower cultivation and chicken rearing programs

  • Oil-producing companies for sunflower seed purchases
  • Agricultural equipment and supplies
  • Training and technical support
  • Market linkages for produce

Impact: Create sustainable income streams and food security for 30 families

Education Sponsorship

Opportunity: Sponsor a child's education and break the cycle of poverty

  • School fees for primary and secondary education
  • School supplies and uniforms
  • Vocational training programs
  • Scholarship funds for higher education

Impact: Keep children in school and create future opportunities

Water Infrastructure

Opportunity: Fund critical water access projects

  • Borehole drilling (full or partial sponsorship)
  • Water tanks for rainwater collection
  • Water catchment systems
  • Maintenance and sustainability programs

Impact: Provide clean water access and save hours of daily labor

Skills Training

Opportunity: Support vocational training programs

  • Traditional basket weaving trainers
  • Training materials and equipment
  • Market development support
  • Business skills workshops

Impact: Empower women with marketable skills and income generation

Healthcare

Opportunity: Support medical camps and wellness programs

  • Medical camp organization and supplies
  • Professional healthcare providers
  • Eye care services and glasses
  • Diabetes screening and management
  • Counseling and mental health support

Impact: Provide essential healthcare to women who otherwise couldn't afford it

Climate Justice

Opportunity: Partner on environmental restoration

  • Tree seedlings for planting programs
  • Bamboo cultivation support
  • Renewable energy systems (solar, biogas)
  • Environmental education programs

Impact: Restore the ecosystem and combat climate change at the local level
